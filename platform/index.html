Platform capabilities

Six core modules that take freight follow-ups off your plate.

1

Automated WhatsApp & Email Check-Ins

Scheduled status check-ins sent automatically via WhatsApp and email on your custom cadence.

  • Zero app installs or portal logins for drivers, brokers, or shipping agents
  • Automatic translation and replies in 22+ languages
  • Complete verified audit trail of every message and timestamp
2

Proactive Exception & Delay Command Center

Continuous monitoring that surfaces delays, silent transporters, and customs holds before detention fees accrue.

  • Dedicated “Need your attention” command center with red/amber/green status indicators
  • Automated follow-up pings when a driver or broker goes silent
  • Instant operations team alerts via WhatsApp and dashboard notifications
3

Unified Sea & Road Command Center

Manage international ocean containers and domestic road deliveries inside a single workspace.

  • Instant switching between Table and Kanban board views (In Transit → Customs → Delivered)
  • Integrated Document Vault storing Bills of Lading and Commercial Invoices alongside each shipment
  • Side-by-side tracking for container numbers, ports, trucks, and local drivers
4

AI Intent Detection & Document Extraction

Eliminate manual data entry and automatically interpret unstructured transporter replies.

  • Smart intent detection for delays, breakdowns, and customs holds
  • Instant data extraction from forwarded shipping emails and documents
5

Transporter & Supplier Performance Analytics

Turn daily tracking history into smarter vendor decisions. Manifest measures how fast your contacts reply and how often their shipments run late.

  • Average reply-time tracking per transporter
  • Historical delay rate percentages for every supplier and carrier
  • Full contact directory with preferred communication languages
6

Team Collaboration & Multi-Currency Billing

Built for growing operations teams and international importers. Invite colleagues with controlled permissions and manage billing in your local currency.

  • Role-based access (Owner, Can Add & Edit, View Only)
  • Bulk actions for exporting CSVs, updating schedules, or sending mass check-ins
  • Full timezone support and pricing displayed in 53 currencies
