Why choose Manifest

Built for modern logistics teams who are tired of chasing transporters, digging through scattered WhatsApp chats, and discovering shipment delays when it is already too late.

The problem

Freight tracking shouldn't mean endless phone calls.

Moving cargo from port to warehouse involves dozens of handoffs: shipping lines, customs brokers, freight forwarders, and local truck drivers. Yet most teams still rely on manual phone calls, personal WhatsApp messages, and outdated Excel spreadsheets just to answer one simple question: "Where is our shipment right now?"

Traditional logistics software tries to fix this by forcing transporters and drivers to download an app or log into a portal, which drivers rarely use, leaving your operations team right back on the phone chasing updates manually.

The Manifest difference

Automation that works the way your transporters already do.

Manifest flips the model. Instead of forcing carriers to change how they work, Manifest automatically reaches out on the channels they already check every minute (WhatsApp and email), turns their replies into live dashboard updates, and alerts your team the moment something goes wrong.

Five reasons teams switch

Less chasing. More control.

Zero app download or portal logins for transporters

Your driver, broker, and shipping agents never need to create an account, remember a password, or install another app. Manifest sends clean, automated check-ins directly to their WhatsApp or email. They reply as they normally would and your dashboard stays up to date automatically.

Proactive delay and customs alerts before demurrage hits

Discovering a delay after a container has sat at the port for days costs thousands in detention and demurrage fees. Manifest monitors check-in schedules continuously and flags late shipments, customs holds, and unresponsive transporters in a dedicated "Need your attention" command center so you can step in early.

Sea freight and local trucks in one workspace

Most tools force you to split international ocean imports from domestic road deliveries. Manifest brings both together: track container numbers, ports, and bills of lading alongside local trucks, drivers, and warehouse destinations, and switch between table and kanban views.

Automated check-ins in 22+ languages

Working with overseas suppliers, international freight agents, or regional drivers shouldn't create communication delays. Manifest sends your check-in messages in 22+ languages, so follow-ups are clear across every leg of your supply chain.

AI-powered setup and supplier performance insights

Stop wasting hours typing shipment details by hand. Forward or paste a shipping email and Manifest extracts container numbers, ports, and supplier details in seconds. Over time, Manifest also tracks response times and delay rates for every transporter and supplier, so you can see who delivers on time and who slows you down.
